# Founder Handoff — Nehemiah Autism Center Website

*For the Center's founder and social media manager. No technical knowledge needed.*

## What is ready

The whole website is built in **English and Amharic**: Home, About, Services, Events, Blog, Gallery, Volunteer, FAQ, Contact and Donate. It uses the words from your content document, and your Amharic exactly as you wrote it. The three services are **Applied Behavior Analysis, Speech and Language Therapy and Food Therapy**, everywhere.

It works on phones and computers, and has been tested for people who use keyboards or screen readers. It has **nothing that moves by itself** (no slideshows, counters or animations), because this is calmer for autistic visitors and families.

**It is not public.** Nothing has been put online. A developer will review it first.

## What the site says it cannot do yet — on purpose

| | What visitors see |
|---|---|
| **Donations** | "Online donations are coming soon", with ways to help today: volunteer, attend events, contact the Center. No payment form, no bank details. |
| **Contact form** | It opens the visitor's own email program with their message ready. It says clearly that **nothing is sent until they press Send**. The website cannot receive messages yet. |
| **Volunteer** | Explains the five roles and sends people to contact you. There is no application form yet, so nobody is told "application submitted" when it was not. |
